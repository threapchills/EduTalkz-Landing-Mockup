The Rationale

Global research consistently shows that teacher quality is the single most important in-school factor influencing learner outcomes. A meta-analysis by the OECD notes that improving teacher professional learning has a greater impact on learner achievement than reducing class size.

Yet, in South Africa, teachers face immense pressure: adapting to evolving curricula, diverse learning needs, and overcrowded classrooms. EduTalkz exists to close this gap by providing a national, scalable solution that equips teachers with resilience and practical tools.

Built on a Proven Model

EduTalkz is not an experiment; it is built on the robust infrastructure of Medtalkz, a well-established platform for medical professionals that has grown to more than 37,000 registered users.

By replicating this model—which already houses a library of over 1,700 accredited lectures—we are bringing weekly high-quality CPD webinars and expert-led talks to the education sector. We know this model works at scale.

Beyond Translation

Research from UNESCO and the World Bank affirms that learning in one’s home language deepens comprehension and improves teaching performance. This is why our multilingual capability is about more than just subtitles—it is about cognitive impact.

By offering content in languages including isiZulu, isiXhosa, Sepedi, and Afrikaans, we ensure that complex pedagogical concepts are fully understood and retained, boosting teacher confidence across linguistic divides.

Vital & Scalable

Teachers are often unable to attend in-person workshops due to cost and geographic constraints. Online learning removes these barriers.

  • Anytime Access: Live or on-demand viewing creates a library for lifelong learning.
  • Mobile First: Optimised for phones, ensuring access in rural and low-resource settings.
  • Cost-Effective: EduTalkz remains free to teachers, ensuring no educator is excluded due to financial constraints.
