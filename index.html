Teaching the Teachers

Educators shape the minds that will define our future, yet they are often the most underserved when it comes to continuous professional learning. Teachers face immense pressure: adapting to evolving curricula, integrating new technologies, and maintaining their own mental wellbeing.

EduTalkz recognises that empowered teachers create inspired learners. By offering continuous professional development (CPD) through accessible, evidence-based webinars, we equip teachers with the resilience and practical tools to thrive.

Unity in Diversity

Language should never be a barrier to learning. The EduTalkz translation functionality enables every talk—live or recorded—to be accessed in any language via subtitles.

  • Equitable access for educators in urban and rural areas.
  • Professional growth across linguistic divides.
  • National scalability serving every teacher in South Africa.

Built on Success

EduTalkz is built on the robust infrastructure of Medtalkz, a platform serving over 37,000 health professionals with 1,700+ accredited lectures. We are replicating this proven model for education—bringing weekly high-quality CPD webinars and an ever-growing library of content to teachers across the continent.

Why Online CPD?

Convenience: No travel costs. No disruption to teaching time. Learn on your phone in low-resource settings.

Quality: Expert-led sessions on STEM, literacy, inclusion, and classroom management.

Cost-Effective: EduTalkz is designed to remain free to teachers, upholding our ethos of open access to education.
